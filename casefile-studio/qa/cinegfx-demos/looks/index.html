Cinematic graphics — look options

Built in v0.4.63 (PR #22, not merged). Every picture and clip is drawn by the app's own look code — the same frames the editor previews and the export writes. Words and photos are the approved Javits material. Stand-ins: “your footage” is the NYPD briefing photo (slowly pushing in in the clips), “a project photo” is the courthouse. In the app: Motion › Look of all cinematic graphics (the whole project), or select a graphic › LOOK (that graphic only).

1 · Whole project + one graphic changed

The project look sets colour set B for everything; the legal graphic was changed on its own to set C (outlined). “Use the project look” on a graphic puts it back.

2a · Ready-made colour sets

Three tested sets per category (A is the approved design), plus a light version (or the dark docket for the paper one) where the design has one. Every set passes text ≥ 4.5:1 and accent ≥ 3:1 against its background.

3 · Backgrounds

Its own background, your footage darkened (see-through), a still from your footage, or a photo from the project, with a Darken setting (40–85%). The paper docket switches to its dark version over a picture; contradiction keeps its own background (its columns fill the screen).

Back to back with a see-through graphic (Digital evidence → Investigative findings over the footage)

An opaque graphic covers the one before it; a see-through one would show it underneath, so the earlier one fades out during the next one's 0.2 s fade-in.

4 · Automatic variation (a switch on the project)

The same graphics as three projects get them: each project its own mix of the tested sets, always the same for that project. Sets you choose by hand win.
